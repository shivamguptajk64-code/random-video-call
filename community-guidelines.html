VibeMatch Safety Standard

Community Guidelines

VibeMatch is built for quick, real-time conversations with people around the world. These guidelines explain what is welcome, what is not allowed, and how we protect live video chat, messages, friend requests, notifications, and profiles.

01

Our Community Principles

VibeMatch connects strangers in seconds, so trust has to be simple, visible, and strict. Users should feel free to meet new people, but never pressured to tolerate behavior that is unsafe, sexual, hateful, manipulative, or invasive.

Consent

Respect a person's choice to talk, skip, stop, mute, block, or leave. Silence is not permission.

Safety

Live video chat should not expose users to nudity, threats, scams, harassment, or shock content.

Authenticity

Use a real account, accurate profile details, and honest intentions. Do not impersonate people or organizations.

Privacy

Private information, screenshots, recordings, and personal data must be handled with care and permission.

02

What Is Not Allowed

Nudity, sexual acts, or explicit behavior

Do not show sexual body parts, perform sexual acts, expose yourself, request sexual content, or push another user into sexual conversation. This applies to video, chat, profile images, names, and interests.

Harassment, bullying, and threats

No insults, intimidation, stalking, humiliation, blackmail, repeated unwanted contact, threats of harm, or pressure after someone says no or leaves.

Hate and dehumanizing conduct

Do not attack people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or other protected traits.

Minor safety violations

Sexual, romantic, exploitative, or grooming behavior involving minors is forbidden. If a user appears underage or unsafe, stop the chat and report immediately.

Spam, scams, and fake activity

No bots, phishing links, fake giveaways, investment tricks, adult-site promotion, malware, repeated advertising, or attempts to move users into unsafe external channels.

Illegal or dangerous content

Do not promote violence, weapons misuse, drugs, self-harm instructions, human trafficking, exploitation, or any activity that creates real-world danger.

03

Live Video Chat Rules

Video chat is the fastest part of VibeMatch, so the standard is strict. Keep your camera appropriate, keep your behavior respectful, and use Start, Next, Stop, Report, and Block whenever needed.

  • Keep your face, background, and behavior appropriate for a public conversation.
  • Do not show nudity, sexual gestures, graphic material, weapons used to intimidate, or shock content.
  • Do not pressure users to reveal their body, location, phone number, social accounts, or private photos.
  • Do not record, livestream, screenshot, or repost another user's video without clear permission.
  • Use Next or Stop when a conversation is uncomfortable. Use Report for abuse or safety concerns.

04

Messages, Friend Requests, and Chat

Messages should continue a conversation, not trap someone in one. The same safety rules apply whether you are in quick chat, full chat, notifications, friend requests, or profile interactions.

  • Send messages only to people who are open to hearing from you.
  • No repeated messages after someone ignores, blocks, declines, or asks you to stop.
  • No sexual requests, abusive language, threats, manipulation, or fake emergency stories.
  • No spam links, external payment requests, suspicious files, or adult-site promotion.
  • Friend requests must be genuine. Do not use them to harass, collect users, or bypass blocks.

05

Profile, Identity, and Interests

Your profile helps VibeMatch match people by country, language, and interests. Keep it honest and safe so other users know who they are meeting.

Allowed

Real name or handle, appropriate profile photo, accurate gender and language choices, and interests that describe what you want to talk about.

Not allowed

Impersonation, sexual profile photos, hateful names, scam identities, fake age claims, misleading location, or content that targets vulnerable users.

06

Privacy and Personal Information

Strangers do not need your private details to have a good conversation. Protect your own information and never expose someone else's.

  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, financial details, government IDs, home address, school details, or private photos.
  • Do not ask another user for sensitive information or pressure them to move to another app.
  • Do not dox, threaten to expose, or publish private information about another person.
  • Do not save or distribute another person's image, voice, messages, or profile details without permission.

07

Reporting and Blocking

If something feels unsafe, use the Report button. Reports help detect harmful patterns across video chat, messages, profiles, and friend activity.

1Stop or skip the chat if you feel uncomfortable.
2Tap Report and choose the closest reason.
3Block the user if you do not want future contact.
4For urgent danger, contact local emergency services first.

False reports can hurt real users and may lead to action against the reporting account.

08

Enforcement

We may take action when an account breaks these guidelines, creates safety risk, or attempts to bypass moderation. Action depends on severity, history, and context.

Warning Feature limit Chat restriction Profile removal Temporary suspension Permanent ban Device or account block Escalation for illegal harm

If you believe action was taken by mistake, contact support with your account details, the approximate time of the incident, and a clear explanation.

Build the kind of room you want to enter.

VibeMatch works best when users meet with curiosity, respect, and control. Be friendly, leave when needed, report harm quickly, and help keep random chat safe for the next person.
